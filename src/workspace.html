About the Access Score

The score starts at 100 and subtracts one weighted deduction per rule with open violations (like Lighthouse, repetition of the same mistake saturates instead of stacking): severity (critical 6 · serious 3 · moderate 1.2 · minor 0.4) × scope (page-wide ×1.5 · interactive element ×1.1 · content ×1 / ×0.7), and the first instance of a rule costs half weight. It is a deterministic triage signal to order your work — not a WCAG certification. Every open violation is visible in the list on the right.
