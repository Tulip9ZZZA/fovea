How Fovea works

1
Render, don't guess

Your page loads in a sandboxed frame. Fovea reads every element's real computed styles — cascade, inheritance and all — instead of approximating from raw HTML.

2
24 WCAG-aligned rules

Contrast via WCAG relative-luminance math with ancestor alpha-compositing, missing names and labels, skipped headings, broken tab order, disabled zoom, and more.

3
X-ray + simulate

Violations pin onto the live elements. Then step into your users' shoes: protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, low-vision tunnel vision, and a keyboard-only walkthrough.

4
Fix it live

Apply a suggested fix to the sandboxed copy and the Access Score recomputes instantly — a before/after record proves what changed, with copy-paste snippets for the real code.

Fovea is a triage and awareness tool built in a hackathon — it does not certify WCAG conformance, and simulations are approximations, not clinical tools. See DECISIONS.md for every deliberate limitation.
